<p>The boulevardier is the negroni's cousin with whisky instead of gin: warmer and spicier. The <strong>non-alcoholic boulevardier</strong> keeps all the character of the original, with no alcohol.</p>
<h2>Ingredients</h2>
<ul><li>40 ml dark 0% spirit (whisky style)</li><li>30 ml 0% bitter</li><li>30 ml 0% red vermouth</li><li>Orange peel</li><li>Ice</li></ul>
<h2>Method</h2>
<p>Pour everything into a mixing glass with ice and stir for 20 seconds. Strain into a chilled coupe and squeeze an orange peel over the rim.</p>
<h2>Tips</h2>
<ul><li>Use plenty of ice and chilled glasses.</li><li>Fresh citrus makes the difference.</li></ul>
