<p><strong>Sambuca</strong> is the Italian star-anise liqueur, sweet and fragrant. The <strong>non-alcoholic</strong> version keeps the anise and fennel character and suits anyone who wants the after-dinner ritual without alcohol.</p>
<h2>How to drink it</h2>
<ul><li><strong>Con la mosca</strong>: 40 ml in a small glass with 3 coffee beans, said to bring luck.</li><li><strong>Ice cold</strong>: kept in the freezer it turns thicker and less sweet.</li><li><strong>In coffee</strong>: 1 tablespoon in an espresso for a 0% &ldquo;corretto&rdquo;.</li></ul>
<h2>Cocktails and pairings</h2>
<p>With tonic water and ice it makes an aromatic long drink. It pairs with dry biscuits, cantucci and dark chocolate. After dinner the <a href="/en/shop/irish-cream-style-0/">0% Irish cream</a> is also excellent.</p>
<h2>FAQ</h2>
<h3>Does non-alcoholic sambuca taste of anise?</h3><p>Yes, it is the main flavour, with sweet fennel notes.</p>
<h3>Why three coffee beans?</h3><p>They stand for health, happiness and prosperity; the coffee balances the sweetness.</p>
