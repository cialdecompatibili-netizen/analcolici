<p>A much-searched question: is <strong>ginger beer alcoholic</strong>? In the vast majority of cases <strong>no</strong>. The ginger beer on sale today is a fizzy ginger soft drink, with an alcohol content of zero or under 0.5%.</p>
<h2>Why the name causes confusion</h2>
<p>Historically ginger beer was a fermented drink, and a small amount of alcohol did form. Today industrial versions are made from ginger, sugar, water and carbon dioxide, with no significant fermentation. Still check the label: it should say «non-alcoholic» or give the percentage.</p>
<h2>Ginger beer vs ginger ale</h2>
<ul><li><strong>Ginger beer</strong>: intense, spicy flavour, cloudy, with more ginger.</li><li><strong>Ginger ale</strong>: sweeter and milder, clear, with a light ginger taste.</li></ul>
<h2>How to use it in mocktails</h2>
<ul><li><a href="/en/blog/mocktail-recipes/ricetta-moscow-mule-analcolico-zenzero-lime/">Non-alcoholic Moscow Mule</a>: ginger beer, lime and ice.</li><li><strong>Dark Stormy</strong>: with 0% dark rum for a stormy effect.</li><li><strong>El Diablo</strong>: with blackcurrant and lime.</li></ul>
<h2>Frequently asked questions</h2>
<h3>Is it suitable for children?</h3><p>Yes, but it can be spicy: taste it first or dilute it with soda.</p>
<h3>Does it contain sugar?</h3><p>Usually yes: to reduce it, choose a light version or mix with lemon soda.</p>
