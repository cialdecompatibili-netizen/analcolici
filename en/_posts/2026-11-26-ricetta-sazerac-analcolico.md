<p>The sazerac is New Orleans' cocktail: dry, spiced, with a hint of anise. The <strong>non-alcoholic sazerac</strong> keeps all the character of the original, with no alcohol.</p>
<h2>Ingredients</h2>
<ul><li>50 ml dark 0% spirit</li><li>1 sugar cube</li><li>3 dashes 0% aromatic bitters</li><li>A few drops of anise essence</li><li>Lemon peel</li></ul>
<h2>Method</h2>
<p>Rinse a chilled glass with anise. In another glass muddle the sugar cube with the bitters, add the spirit and ice and stir. Strain into the rinsed glass and finish with the peel.</p>
<h2>Tips</h2>
<ul><li>Use plenty of ice and chilled glasses.</li><li>Fresh citrus makes the difference.</li></ul>
