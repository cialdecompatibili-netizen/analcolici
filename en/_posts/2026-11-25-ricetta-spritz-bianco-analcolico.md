<p>The <strong>white spritz</strong> is the pale, delicate version of the best-loved aperitif: no red bitter, just a base of <strong>herbs, flowers and citrus</strong> lengthened with bubbles. Non-alcoholic, it stays fresh, slightly bitter and perfect for anyone who finds the classic spritz too sweet.</p>
<h2>Ingredients (1 glass)</h2>
<ul><li>60 ml white 0% aperitif</li><li>90 ml cold 0% sparkling wine</li><li>30 ml soda</li><li>Ice, a lemon slice and a rosemary sprig</li></ul>
<h2>Method</h2>
<p>Fill a glass with ice. Pour the white aperitif, then the 0% sparkling and finally the soda. Stir once from bottom to top and garnish with lemon and rosemary.</p>
<h2>White spritz or classic spritz?</h2>
<ul><li><strong>Colour</strong>: pale straw yellow versus orange.</li><li><strong>Taste</strong>: herbal and floral versus bitter and citrusy.</li><li><strong>When</strong>: ideal in summer and at light lunches.</li></ul>
<p>For the classic, read the <a href="/en/blog/mocktail-recipes/ricetta-spritz-agrumato-analcolico/">citrus spritz recipe</a>.</p>
<h2>Pairings</h2>
<p>With <strong>crudités</strong>, <strong>raw fish</strong>, <strong>mozzarella</strong> and <strong>lemon canapés</strong>.</p>
<h2>Frequently asked questions</h2>
<h3>Does a white spritz taste sweet?</h3><p>It is less sweet than the classic: the bitterness is softer and more floral.</p>
<h3>Can I use tonic instead of soda?</h3><p>Yes, for a more bitter finish; reduce the aperitif by 10 ml.</p>
