<p>A <strong>mocktail</strong> is a cocktail without alcohol, built with the same logic as a traditional drink: an aromatic base, a sour element, a sweet one and often something sparkling. The name blends <em>mock</em> (fake, imitation) and <em>cocktail</em>, but today "fake" undersells it: a good mocktail is a complete drink, made to be enjoyed as much as an alcoholic one.</p>

<h2>Mocktail, cocktail and soft drink: the differences</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Cocktail</strong>: contains an alcoholic part (spirit, liqueur or wine).</li>
<li><strong>Mocktail</strong>: copies the structure of a cocktail, without alcohol. Made with juices, syrups, bitters and 0% spirits.</li>
<li><strong>Soft drink</strong>: a ready-made beverage like cola or orange soda, without the complexity of a mixology recipe.</li>
</ul>
<p>Read more in the guide on the <a href="/en/blog/aperitif-guides/differenza-aperitivo-analcolico-mocktail/">difference between a non-alcoholic aperitif and a mocktail</a>.</p>

<h2>How a mocktail is built</h2>
<p>The structure is always the same, four balanced elements:</p>
<ol>
<li><strong>Base</strong>: juice, iced tea, non-alcoholic spirit or infusion.</li>
<li><strong>Acidity</strong>: lime, lemon, grapefruit.</li>
<li><strong>Sweetness</strong>: syrup, honey, fruit.</li>
<li><strong>Fizz and aroma</strong>: soda, tonic, ginger beer, fresh herbs.</li>
</ol>

<h2>Why they are more and more popular</h2>
<p>Drivers, people who don't drink by choice, those joining months like <a href="/en/blog/trends-culture/dry-january-damp-january-differenza/">Dry January</a> or simply alternating drinks with and without alcohol: mocktails fit all of these without giving up the aperitif ritual.</p>

<h2>Frequently asked questions</h2>
<h3>Does a mocktail really contain zero alcohol?</h3>
<p>Yes, if made with non-alcoholic ingredients. Always check product labels, since "non-alcoholic" in some countries allows trace amounts.</p>
<h3>Can I drink a mocktail at any hour?</h3>
<p>Yes, but watch the sugar: many recipes use juices and syrups, so alternate them with water.</p>
<h3>Which mocktail is best for beginners?</h3>
<p>The non-alcoholic mojito: common ingredients, no special tools and a fresh taste everyone likes.</p>
