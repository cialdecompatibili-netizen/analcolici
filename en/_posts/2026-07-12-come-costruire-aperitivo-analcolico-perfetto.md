<p>A successful non-alcoholic aperitif is not just a question of the drink: it is a small ritual made up of details. Here's how to build it step by step, from the right glass to the pairings on the table.</p><h2>The right glass</h2><p>A large wine glass enhances the aromas of aperitifs 0% more than a simple tumbler. For long drinks, however, a tall glass leaves room for ice and keeps the drink cold for longer.</p><h2>Ice: quantity and quality</h2><p>The ice is not a secondary detail: abundant and of good quality (without extraneous flavors from the freezer) it maintains the temperature without diluting the drink too quickly.</p><h2>Garnish: less is more</h2><p>A slice of citrus fruit, a sprig of fresh herb or a peel: the garnish must complement the aroma, not cover it. Avoid decorative excesses that add nothing to the taste.</p><h2>Pairings on the table</h2><p>Bitter non-alcoholic aperitifs go well with platters of mature cheeses and olives; the fruitier versions go better with bruschetta and light finger foods.</p>

<!-- ext2 --><h2>The aperitif snack board</h2><ul><li><strong>Salty</strong>: olives, taralli, nuts, veggie crisps.</li><li><strong>Fresh</strong>: crudités with hummus, mozzarella bites, cherry tomatoes.</li><li><strong>Bold</strong>: cured meats and aged cheeses, which pair with bitter drinks.</li></ul><h2>How many drinks to prepare</h2><p>Plan 2-3 glasses per person over two hours. Prepare ice, cut citrus and syrups ahead so you can serve in seconds.</p><h2>Low alcohol vs non-alcoholic</h2><p>A <em>low alcohol</em> drink has reduced strength but not zero; a non-alcoholic (0%) one has none. For an aperitif suitable for everyone, drivers and non-drinkers, choose 0%. For the full difference read <a href="/en/blog/aperitif-guides/differenza-aperitivo-analcolico-mocktail/">non-alcoholic aperitif or mocktail</a>.</p><h2>Three ready drinks</h2><p>Start with a <a href="/en/blog/mocktail-recipes/ricetta-spritz-agrumato-analcolico/">citrus spritz</a>, add a <a href="/en/blog/mocktail-recipes/ricetta-negroni-analcolico-amaro-agrumato/">non-alcoholic negroni</a> for bitter lovers and a <a href="/en/blog/mocktail-recipes/ricetta-mojito-analcolico-menta-lime/">non-alcoholic mojito</a> for something fresh.</p><h2>FAQ</h2><h3>How far ahead can I prepare?</h3><p>Syrups and juices the day before; ice and bubbles only at the last moment.</p><h3>Which glasses do I need?</h3><p>Wine glasses, tumblers and a few coupes cover every recipe.</p>
