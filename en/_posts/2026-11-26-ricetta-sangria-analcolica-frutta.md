<p>It is the ultimate sharing drink: make it ahead and it improves with resting. The <strong>non-alcoholic sangria</strong> keeps all the character of the original, with no alcohol.</p>
<h2>Ingredients</h2>
<ul><li>500 ml dealcoholised red wine</li><li>1 orange, sliced</li><li>1 apple, diced</li><li>1 lemon, sliced</li><li>2 tbsp cane sugar</li><li>Soda and cinnamon</li></ul>
<h2>Method</h2>
<p>Put fruit, sugar and cinnamon in a pitcher, pour in the dealcoholised wine and rest in the fridge at least 2 hours. Serve over ice with a splash of soda.</p>
<h2>Tips</h2>
<ul><li>Use plenty of ice and chilled glasses.</li><li>Fresh citrus makes the difference.</li></ul>

<h3>How long does it keep?</h3><p>Up to 24 hours: after that the fruit loses freshness.</p>
