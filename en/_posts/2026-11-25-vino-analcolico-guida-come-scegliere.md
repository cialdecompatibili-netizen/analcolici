<p><strong>Non-alcoholic wine</strong> is wine from which the alcohol is removed after fermentation, keeping as much aroma, acidity and structure as possible. It is not grape juice: it is a drink meant to accompany meals like wine, without the effects of alcohol.</p>
<h2>How it is made</h2>
<p>The wine is made in the traditional way and then dealcoholised with gentle techniques, such as vacuum distillation or filtration. The aromas are preserved, but the body ends up lighter.</p>
<h2>How to choose</h2>
<ul><li><strong>Red 0%</strong>: red fruit and soft tannin, for meat and aged cheese.</li><li><strong>White 0%</strong>: fresh and floral, for fish and starters.</li><li><strong>Rosé 0%</strong>: strawberry and freshness, for aperitifs and summer dishes.</li></ul>
<p>You can see the versions in our <a href="/en/shop/category/wine-0/">0% wine catalogue</a>.</p>
<h2>How to serve it</h2>
<ul><li><strong>Temperature</strong>: red at 16-18°C, white and rosé at 8-10°C.</li><li><strong>Glass</strong>: a wide glass to release the aromas.</li><li><strong>Once opened</strong>: in the fridge, to be drunk within 2-3 days.</li></ul>
<h2>Pairings</h2>
<p>Red with <strong>ragù pasta</strong> and <strong>roasts</strong>, white with <strong>fish</strong> and <strong>vegetables</strong>, rosé with <strong>pizza</strong> and <strong>sushi</strong>.</p>
<h2>Frequently asked questions</h2>
<h3>Does non-alcoholic wine really have zero alcohol?</h3><p>Check the label: 0% versions contain no alcohol, others may have up to 0.5%.</p>
<h3>Is it suitable during pregnancy?</h3><p>Ask your doctor: even small traces of alcohol may be discouraged.</p>
