<p>It's called 'zebra striping' and it's one of the most interesting trends that have emerged in the European drinking scene: alternating, on the same occasion, an alcoholic and a non-alcoholic drink. Like the stripes of a zebra, precisely: black and white, one after the other.</p><h2>It's not abstinence, it's style</h2><p>Unlike Dry January, which focuses on an entire month of zero alcohol, zebra striping does not require total renunciation. It's rather a way to stay clear for longer during the evening, enjoy the ritual of toasting without accumulating alcohol, and maintain control over how you feel.</p><h2>Because it works with a 0% menu</h2><p>For those who frequent clubs or organize evenings at home, having mocktails of a quality comparable to alcoholic cocktails available is what makes zebra striping possible: no one wants to go from a real Spritz to a banal fruit juice. The change must be imperceptible in taste, clear only in gradation.</p>

<!-- ext2 --><h2>How to do zebra striping</h2><ol><li>Alternate an alcoholic glass with a <strong>non-alcoholic</strong> one or water.</li><li>Pick 0% drinks similar to the original so you don't feel left out.</li><li>Set a limit before going out and stick to it.</li></ol><h2>Practical benefits</h2><ul><li>You drink more slowly and stay hydrated.</li><li>You feel better the next day.</li><li>You can stay out longer without giving up the company.</li></ul><h2>Which drinks to use</h2><p>The trick is alternating with 0% versions of the classics: a <a href="/en/blog/mocktail-recipes/ricetta-spritz-agrumato-analcolico/">non-alcoholic spritz</a>, a <a href="/en/blog/mocktail-recipes/ricetta-mojito-analcolico-menta-lime/">non-alcoholic mojito</a> or a <a href="/en/blog/mocktail-recipes/ricetta-bellini-analcolico-pesca-veneto/">non-alcoholic bellini</a>.</p><h2>FAQ</h2><h3>What is zebra striping?</h3><p>It is the habit of alternating alcoholic and non-alcoholic drinks in the same evening.</p><h3>Is it the same as damp january?</h3><p>They are related ideas: read <a href="/en/blog/trends-culture/dry-january-damp-january-differenza/">dry january and damp january</a>.</p>
