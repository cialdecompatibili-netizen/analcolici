<p>It's called 'zebra striping' and it's one of the most interesting trends that have emerged in the European drinking scene: alternating, on the same occasion, an alcoholic and a non-alcoholic drink. Like the stripes of a zebra, precisely: black and white, one after the other.</p><h2>It's not abstinence, it's style</h2><p>Unlike Dry January, which focuses on an entire month of zero alcohol, zebra striping does not require total renunciation. It's rather a way to stay clear for longer during the evening, enjoy the ritual of toasting without accumulating alcohol, and maintain control over how you feel.</p><h2>Because it works with a 0% menu</h2><p>For those who frequent clubs or organize evenings at home, having mocktails of a quality comparable to alcoholic cocktails available is what makes zebra striping possible: no one wants to go from a real Spritz to a banal fruit juice. The change must be imperceptible in taste, clear only in gradation.</p>
