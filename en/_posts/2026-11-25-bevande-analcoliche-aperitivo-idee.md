<p>Aperitif is not just spritz. If you are looking for a <strong>non-alcoholic aperitif drink</strong>, there are many options: what matters is a <strong>dry, slightly bitter</strong> taste that opens the appetite. Here are eight ideas.</p>
<h2>The 8 ideas</h2>
<ol><li><strong>Bitter soda</strong>: non-alcoholic bitter and soda, the simplest and driest.</li><li><strong>Spritz 0%</strong>: orange and bubbles, the classic.</li><li><a href="/en/blog/mocktail-recipes/ricetta-spritz-bianco-analcolico/">White spritz</a>: more floral and less sweet.</li><li><strong>Vermouth and soda 0%</strong>: herbs and citrus, Turin style.</li><li><strong>Americano 0%</strong>: bitter and vermouth with soda.</li><li><strong>Non-alcoholic gin and tonic</strong>: juniper and premium tonic.</li><li><strong>Negroni 0%</strong>: for lovers of intense bitterness, like the <a href="/en/blog/mocktail-recipes/ricetta-negroni-analcolico-amaro-agrumato/">non-alcoholic negroni</a>.</li><li><strong>Kombucha</strong>: tart and fermented, see the <a href="/en/blog/wellness-detox/kombucha-fermentati-aperitivo-analcolico/">guide to ferments</a>.</li></ol>
<h2>How to serve them</h2>
<ul><li>Cold glass and plenty of ice.</li><li>A citrus peel or slice for aroma.</li><li>Little sugar: a dry taste pairs better with snacks.</li></ul>
<h2>What to pair them with</h2>
<p>Olives, crisps, taralli, cubed cheese, cured meats and canapés. To build a full aperitif read the <a href="/en/blog/aperitif-guides/come-costruire-aperitivo-analcolico-perfetto/">guide to the non-alcoholic aperitif</a>.</p>
<h2>Frequently asked questions</h2>
<h3>Which non-alcoholic drink is closest to the classic aperitif?</h3><p>The 0% spritz and the bitter soda, which echo the orange and bitter aromas.</p>
<h3>Can they be prepared ahead?</h3><p>The bases yes; add the bubbles at the last moment.</p>
