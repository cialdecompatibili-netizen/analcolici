<p>Making a good <strong>non-alcoholic cocktail</strong> at home is easier than it looks. You don't need a pro shaker or ten bottles: ice, fresh citrus, something sparkling and a quality base are enough. Here are six simple recipes, each with fewer than five ingredients and ready in five minutes.</p>

<h2>Three rules for a mocktail that always works</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Plenty of clean ice</strong>: the colder the glass, the less the drink dilutes.</li>
<li><strong>Freshly squeezed citrus</strong>: bottled juice flattens the flavour.</li>
<li><strong>Balance sweet, sour and bitter</strong>: if the drink is cloying add lime, if it is too dry add a splash of syrup.</li>
</ul>

<h2>1. Non-alcoholic spritz</h2>
<p>The most requested of all. Fill a glass with ice, pour 60 ml of non-alcoholic bitter base, 90 ml of sparkling drink and a splash of soda. Garnish with an orange slice. The ready-made version is the <a href="/en/shop/spritz-0/">Spritz 0%</a>, and the <a href="/en/blog/aperitif-guides/come-costruire-aperitivo-analcolico-perfetto/">guide to the perfect non-alcoholic aperitif</a> explains how to build a full evening.</p>

<h2>2. Non-alcoholic mojito</h2>
<p>Muddle 8 mint leaves with 2 teaspoons of cane sugar and the juice of half a lime. Add crushed ice and top with sparkling water. Full details in the <a href="/en/blog/mocktail-recipes/ricetta-mojito-analcolico-menta-lime/">non-alcoholic mojito recipe</a>.</p>

<h2>3. Non-alcoholic Moscow Mule</h2>
<p>Fill a glass with ice, add the juice of half a lime and 120 ml of very cold ginger beer. Refreshing, spicy and no technique required. See the <a href="/en/blog/mocktail-recipes/ricetta-moscow-mule-analcolico-zenzero-lime/">recipe with measures</a>.</p>

<h2>4. Non-alcoholic gin and tonic</h2>
<p>Ice, 50 ml of botanical 0% spirit, 120 ml of premium tonic water and a cucumber slice or lemon peel. The secret is the tonic: a good one changes everything.</p>

<h2>5. Non-alcoholic Cosmopolitan</h2>
<p>Shake 60 ml of cranberry juice, 20 ml of lime juice and 15 ml of orange syrup with ice, then strain into a chilled coupe. More in the <a href="/en/blog/mocktail-recipes/ricetta-cosmopolitan-analcolico-mirtillo-rosso/">non-alcoholic cosmopolitan</a> recipe.</p>

<h2>6. Non-alcoholic Bellini</h2>
<p>Two fingers of peach puree in a flute, topped with very cold non-alcoholic bubbles. Elegant and sweet, perfect for an alcohol-free toast: the <a href="/en/blog/mocktail-recipes/ricetta-bellini-analcolico-pesca-veneto/">bellini recipe</a> explains how to pick the peaches.</p>

<h2>Frequently asked questions</h2>
<h3>Which non-alcoholic cocktails are the easiest to make?</h3>
<p>Spritz, mojito and moscow mule: they only need ice, a citrus fruit and something sparkling, no shaker.</p>
<h3>Do I need special equipment?</h3>
<p>No. A tall glass, a long spoon and a knife cover almost every recipe. A shaker is only needed for cosmopolitan-style drinks.</p>
<h3>Can I prepare them ahead?</h3>
<p>The bases (syrups, squeezed juices) yes, even the day before. The sparkling part should always be added at the last moment.</p>
