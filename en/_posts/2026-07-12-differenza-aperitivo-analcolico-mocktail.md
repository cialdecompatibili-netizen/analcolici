<p>The terms are often used synonymously, but there are useful differences to know between 'non-alcoholic aperitif' and 'mocktail', especially if you want to choose the right product for the right occasion.</p><h2>Non-alcoholic aperitif: ready to pour</h2><p>By non-alcoholic aperitif we generally mean a ready-made drink, formulated to be poured over ice and diluted with tonic water or soda: quick, without the need for bar equipment.</p><h2>Mocktail: the 'artisanal' component</h2><p>The mocktail, on the other hand, is a real cocktail in its technique: shaken, mixed, garnished, often with multiple ingredients (syrups, fresh juices, non-alcoholic bitters) combined at the moment.</p><h2>Which one to choose</h2><p>For a quick aperitif with friends, a good ready-made product solves the problem in a few seconds. For a special occasion, dedicating a few extra minutes to preparing a mocktail offers a more refined, almost bartender-like experience.</p>

<!-- ext2 --><h2>Mocktail meaning</h2><p>The word <strong>mocktail</strong> joins <em>mock</em> (fake) and <em>cocktail</em>: a cocktail without alcohol, built like a real drink with juices, syrups, soda and spices. You may also see it spelled <em>mocktails</em> or, by mistake, <em>moktail</em>.</p><h2>Quick differences</h2><ul><li><strong>Mocktail</strong>: more creative and sweeter, often fruity.</li><li><strong>Non-alcoholic aperitif</strong>: bitter, dry flavour meant to open the appetite before dinner.</li><li><strong>0% drink</strong>: ready-made industrial product without alcohol.</li></ul><h2>Which to choose</h2><p>For a fruity after-dinner drink pick a mocktail such as the <a href="/en/blog/mocktail-recipes/ricetta-cosmopolitan-analcolico-mirtillo-rosso/">non-alcoholic cosmopolitan</a>. For aperitif time go for a spritz or a <a href="/en/blog/mocktail-recipes/ricetta-negroni-analcolico-amaro-agrumato/">non-alcoholic negroni</a>.</p><h2>FAQ</h2><h3>Is it a non alcoholic cocktail? What does it mean?</h3><p>Yes: a non-alcoholic cocktail is a 0% cocktail, i.e. a mocktail.</p><h3>Can a mocktail contain some alcohol?</h3><p>It depends on the ingredients: dealcoholised products may contain traces, always check the label.</p>
