<p>The <strong>vodka soda</strong> is the most essential drink there is: spirit, sparkling water and a squeeze of citrus. The <strong>non-alcoholic</strong> version uses a neutral 0% spirit for a light, dry long drink with almost no sugar.</p>
<h2>Ingredients (1 tall glass)</h2>
<ul><li>50 ml neutral 0% spirit</li><li>100-120 ml very cold soda</li><li>Juice of half a lemon</li><li>1 lime wedge</li><li>Large ice cubes</li></ul>
<h2>Method</h2>
<p>Chill the glass, fill it with ice, pour in the spirit and the lemon juice. Add the soda <strong>slowly</strong> down the spoon to keep the bubbles. Stir once from the bottom and garnish with lime.</p>
<h2>Tips</h2>
<ul><li><strong>Ice-cold soda</strong>: keep it in the fridge, never at room temperature.</li><li><strong>Large ice</strong>: melts slowly and doesn't dilute.</li><li><strong>Fresh citrus</strong>: squeeze the peel over the rim for aroma.</li></ul>
<h2>Variations</h2>
<p>Try pink grapefruit, cucumber or a basil leaf. For a more bitter drink see the <a href="/en/blog/mocktail-recipes/ricetta-negroni-analcolico-amaro-agrumato/">non-alcoholic negroni</a>, for a sweeter one the <a href="/en/blog/mocktail-recipes/ricetta-mojito-analcolico-menta-lime/">non-alcoholic mojito</a>.</p>
<h2>FAQ</h2>
<h3>Does a non-alcoholic vodka soda have calories?</h3><p>Very few: with no added sugar, only the citrus juice contributes.</p>
<h3>Can I use tonic water?</h3><p>Yes, but it becomes a vodka tonic: sweeter and slightly bitter.</p>
