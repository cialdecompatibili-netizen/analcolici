<p>A well-made non-alcoholic spritz owes nothing, in terms of pleasure, to the alcoholic version. The key is the balance between bitter, citrus and the right effervescence.</p><h2>Ingredients (1 glass)</h2><ul><li>90 ml of non-alcoholic aperitif 0% bitter-citrus flavour</li><li>60 ml of tonic water or soda</li><li>A slice of orange</li><li>Abundant ice</li></ul><h2>Procedure</h2><p>Fill a wine glass with plenty of ice. Pour the non-alcoholic aperitif, complete with tonic water, mixing delicately with a bar spoon from bottom to top, so as not to disperse the bubbles. Garnish with the orange slice and serve immediately.</p><h2>Additional advice</h2><p>For a fresher note, add two or three lightly crushed mint leaves before pouring in the ice: they will release the aroma without being intrusive.</p>

<h2>Non-alcoholic spritz variations</h2><ul><li><strong>White spritz</strong>: use elderflower syrup and dry dealcoholised sparkling wine.</li><li><strong>Pink spritz</strong>: add 20 ml pink grapefruit juice.</li><li><strong>Herbal spritz</strong>: rosemary or thyme instead of the orange slice.</li></ul><h2>The right ratios</h2><p>The classic rule is <strong>3-2-1</strong>: 3 parts bubbles, 2 parts 0% bitter, 1 part soda. Fill the glass with ice before pouring, and stir only once.</p><h2>Non-alcoholic spritz and white spritz</h2><p>If you search for <em>nonalcoholic spritz</em>, <em>non alcoholic spritz</em> or <em>white spritz</em> you find the same family of drinks: bubbles, bitter aperitif and citrus. For products see the <a href="/en/shop/spritz-bianco-0/">0% White Spritz product page</a>.</p><h2>FAQ</h2><h3>Which glass should I use?</h3><p>A tulip glass or a wide tumbler, with plenty of ice.</p><h3>Which slice?</h3><p>Orange for the classic, lemon or grapefruit for a drier note.</p><h3>Aperitif ideas?</h3><p>Read how to <a href="/en/blog/aperitif-guides/come-costruire-aperitivo-analcolico-perfetto/">build the perfect non-alcoholic aperitif</a>.</p>
