<p>A well-made non-alcoholic spritz owes nothing, in terms of pleasure, to the alcoholic version. The key is the balance between bitter, citrus and the right effervescence.</p><h2>Ingredients (1 glass)</h2><ul><li>90 ml of non-alcoholic aperitif 0% bitter-citrus flavour</li><li>60 ml of tonic water or soda</li><li>A slice of orange</li><li>Abundant ice</li></ul><h2>Procedure</h2><p>Fill a wine glass with plenty of ice. Pour the non-alcoholic aperitif, complete with tonic water, mixing delicately with a bar spoon from bottom to top, so as not to disperse the bubbles. Garnish with the orange slice and serve immediately.</p><h2>Additional advice</h2><p>For a fresher note, add two or three lightly crushed mint leaves before pouring in the ice: they will release the aroma without being intrusive.</p>
