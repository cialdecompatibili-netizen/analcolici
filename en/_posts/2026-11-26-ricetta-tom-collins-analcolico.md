<p>A refreshing long classic: tarter than a gin and tonic and with no bitterness. The <strong>non-alcoholic tom collins</strong> keeps all the character of the original, with no alcohol.</p>
<h2>Ingredients</h2>
<ul><li>50 ml 0% juniper spirit</li><li>25 ml lemon juice</li><li>15 ml simple syrup</li><li>Soda</li><li>Ice and lemon slice</li></ul>
<h2>Method</h2>
<p>In a tall glass with ice pour spirit, lemon and syrup. Top with soda and stir gently. Garnish with a lemon slice.</p>
<h2>Tips</h2>
<ul><li>Use plenty of ice and chilled glasses.</li><li>Fresh citrus makes the difference.</li></ul>
