<p>The Hugo comes from South Tyrol: elderflower and mint make it floral and fresh. The <strong>non-alcoholic hugo</strong> keeps all the character of the original, with no alcohol.</p>
<h2>Ingredients</h2>
<ul><li>60 ml dealcoholised sparkling wine</li><li>20 ml elderflower syrup</li><li>Mint leaves</li><li>Soda</li><li>Lime slice</li></ul>
<h2>Method</h2>
<p>Lightly press the mint in the glass, add ice and the elderflower syrup. Pour in the dealcoholised sparkling wine and a splash of soda. Garnish with lime.</p>
<h2>Tips</h2>
<ul><li>Use plenty of ice and chilled glasses.</li><li>Fresh citrus makes the difference.</li></ul>
