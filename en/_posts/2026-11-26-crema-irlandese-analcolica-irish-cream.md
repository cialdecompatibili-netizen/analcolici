<p><strong>Irish cream</strong> is a creamy liqueur made with milk, cream, cocoa and vanilla. The <strong>non-alcoholic</strong> version keeps the same roundness and the coffee-chocolate flavour, without alcohol: perfect after dinner or in desserts.</p>
<h2>How to serve it</h2>
<ul><li><strong>Neat over ice</strong>: 50 ml in a short glass.</li><li><strong>In coffee</strong>: 2 tablespoons in a long espresso for a 0% Irish coffee.</li><li><strong>Blended</strong>: with ice and a pinch of cocoa it becomes an adult milkshake.</li></ul>
<h2>Recipe: non-alcoholic Irish coffee</h2>
<p>Heat a long coffee, add 40 ml of 0% Irish cream and stir. Top with lightly whipped cream and bitter cocoa. Serve hot in a handled glass.</p>
<h2>In desserts</h2>
<p>It works in tiramisu, over vanilla ice cream and in bundt cakes. For a more structured drink see the <a href="/en/blog/mocktail-recipes/ricetta-negroni-analcolico-amaro-agrumato/">non-alcoholic negroni</a> or the <a href="/en/shop/sambuca-0/">non-alcoholic sambuca</a> to pair after dinner.</p>
<h2>FAQ</h2>
<h3>Does 0% Irish cream keep?</h3><p>Once opened, keep it in the fridge and use it within a few days, since it contains dairy.</p>
<h3>Does it taste like the alcoholic one?</h3><p>Very similar: the warmth of alcohol is missing, but cocoa, vanilla and creaminess remain.</p>
