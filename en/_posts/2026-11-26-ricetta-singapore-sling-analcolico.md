<p>Born in Singapore around 1915, it is fruity and rosy: perfect for tropical-drink lovers. The <strong>non-alcoholic singapore sling</strong> keeps all the character of the original, with no alcohol.</p>
<h2>Ingredients</h2>
<ul><li>40 ml 0% juniper spirit</li><li>60 ml pineapple juice</li><li>15 ml lime juice</li><li>10 ml pomegranate syrup</li><li>Soda and cherry</li></ul>
<h2>Method</h2>
<p>Shake spirit, pineapple, lime and syrup with ice. Pour into a tall glass, top with soda and garnish with the cherry.</p>
<h2>Tips</h2>
<ul><li>Use plenty of ice and chilled glasses.</li><li>Fresh citrus makes the difference.</li></ul>
