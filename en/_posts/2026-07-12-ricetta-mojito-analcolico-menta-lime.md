<p>The mojito is one of the most loved long drinks in the world, and its non-alcoholic version loses nothing in freshness. The secret lies in the mint preparation technique.</p><h2>Ingredients (1 glass)</h2><ul><li>8-10 fresh mint leaves</li><li>20ml fresh lime juice</li><li>15ml brown sugar syrup</li><li>Soda or sparkling water to taste</li><li>Crushed ice</li></ul><h2>Procedure</h2><p>Place the mint leaves at the bottom of the glass along with the lime and syrup. Crush gently with a pestle, without crushing the mint too much (it risks becoming bitter). Fill with crushed ice, top with soda and stir from bottom to top. Garnish with a sprig of fresh mint and a slice of lime.</p><h2>Because it works</h2><p>The delicate crushing releases the essential oils of the mint without extracting the bitterness of the leaves broken too deeply: it is the difference between a balanced mojito and a cloying one.</p>
